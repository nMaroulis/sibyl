## Account and execution scope

New workspaces have no active accounts. Multiple exchanges can be active together; optional Demo is explicit and turns off when an exchange activates. Test funds are separately identified and excluded from production totals. Market and research source settings are independent of account activation.

Twenty production exchange adapters share an interface, but their order, history and automation coverage differs. The Settings feature dialog reflects the implementation for the selected provider/environment. Balance-read access does not establish trading permission. See [exchange coverage](../developers/exchange-clients.md) and [acceptance evidence](../developers/exchange-acceptance.md).

Paper strategies use isolated virtual capital even with real market prices. Exchange strategies require account-bound approval and both execution flags. Manual exchange orders have their own review. AI agents cannot submit orders or access credentials.

## Evidence and accounting scope

Live sources retain visible errors and stale states rather than substituting Demo fixtures. Retained fill FIFO, transfer assertions and native-asset balance checks describe the evidence imported into this workspace; they do not establish complete account history or tax reporting.

Funding imports support Binance crypto deposits/withdrawals, Kraken posted deposit/withdrawal/transfer ledgers and Bybit master-UID on-chain withdrawals and same-UID transfers into or out of UNIFIED. Pending updates and recent scans are opt-in. Observed versions preserve changes seen locally, not a provider's complete revision history. See [funding contracts](../developers/funding-history.md) and [recent sync](../developers/funding-recent-sync.md).

Transfer review can carry acquisition lots across eligible confirmed pairs, including explicit balanced same-asset fee allocations. Across pairs now adds dated retained-price conversion, cross-market FIFO and explicit split/merged groups. Broader fee/wallet policies and provider-verified complete P&L remain pending. New measured performance periods support explicitly reviewed cash flows and approximate returns; they do not supply missing historical conversions or independently verified funding coverage. See [transfer cost basis](../developers/transfer-cost-basis.md).

## Analysis and AI scope

Backtests retain their inputs and assumptions, support exact-input reruns with the current engine, and show simulated executions. Holdout/walk-forward validation uses chronological windows, but repeated experimentation can still overfit. The technical-bias score measures mechanical direction; news sentiment measures supplied wording; Chronos separates fitted ridge forecasts with held-out evaluation from its untrained baseline; neither guarantees future coverage or profitability.

Sibyl requires a configured API/local model and consults specialists through native ProtoLink discovery/delegation. Page dialogs retain direct specialist access and factual tool mode. Telegram, Discord, Slack, Signal, WhatsApp and email have checked private bindings and durable text delivery. Alert rules can select any of them; scheduled text briefings can also select any of them, with Telegram-only documents. Initial Telegram conversation acceptance is user-confirmed; broader live provider/processor acceptance remains a deployment check. See [Sibyl](sibyl.md) and [Telegram](telegram.md).

## Deployment scope

The current application is a single-user, single-worker workspace. Multi-user hosting is deferred and remains visible in Coming soon. Follow the [deployment guide](../developers/deployment.md) for token, persistence and network requirements.

Historical analysis also includes [current-holding basket risk](basket-risk.md), with explicit subset coverage and frozen daily history, and [continuous walk-forward replay](strategy-validation.md#continuous-walk-forward-replay), with one carried simulated balance. These do not establish actual historical account performance or deploy a strategy. [Telegram delivery review](telegram.md#review-alert-and-briefing-delivery) covers grouped conversation replies as well as alerts and briefings.
